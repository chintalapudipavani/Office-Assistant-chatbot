# Leave and Attendance Policy
Brightwave Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version 2.4 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: This is a FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created only for testing a software project. It is not a real company policy and must not be used for real HR decisions.

## 1. Annual Leave
- Every employee gets 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at 1.5 days per month. Employees who join during the year receive leave in proportion to the months remaining.
- Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Any additional unused days lapse on 31 December.
- Leave encashment is available only at resignation, for a maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave.

## 2. Sick Leave
- Every employee gets 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Inform your manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of sick leave.
- A medical certificate is required if sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

## 3. Casual Leave
- Every employee gets 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for urgent personal matters.
- Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Special Leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
- Marriage leave: 5 days, taken once during employment.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days on the death of an immediate family member.

## 5. How to Apply for Leave
- Apply through the PeopleDesk portal.
- Annual leave of more than 2 days must be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
- Managers must approve or reject a request within 2 working days.
- For emergencies, call or message your manager first and then apply in PeopleDesk within 2 working days of returning.

## 6. Attendance Rules
- Employees mark attendance through the PeopleDesk mobile app or the biometric reader at reception.
- A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed after 9:30 AM, up to 3 late arrivals per month.
- From the 4th late arrival in a month, half a day of casual leave is deducted for each late arrival.
- Working fewer than 8 hours counts as a half day, and working fewer than 4 hours counts as an absence for the day.
- Being absent for 3 consecutive days without informing the manager leads to a written notice from HR.

## 7. Public Holidays 2026
The company observes 9 paid public holidays in 2026:
- 1 January: New Year's Day
- 26 January: Republic Day
- 14 March: Spring Festival
- 1 May: Labour Day
- 15 August: Independence Day
- 2 October: Gandhi Jayanti
- 9 November and 10 November: Festival of Lights
- 25 December: Christmas Day
Employees may also choose 2 optional holidays per year from a floating list published by HR in January.
