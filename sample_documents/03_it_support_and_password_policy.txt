# IT Support and Password Security Policy
Brightwave Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version 1.8 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: This is a FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created only for testing a software project. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. IT Help Desk
- Email: ithelp@brightwave.example. Phone: extension 100.
- Help desk hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Raise tickets in the TechAssist portal.
- Response targets: P1 (system down for many people) within 30 minutes, P2 (one person cannot work) within 4 hours, P3 (minor issue or request) within 2 business days.

## 2. Password Rules
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with an authenticator app is mandatory for all accounts.

## 3. Resetting a Password
- Use the self-service reset page at reset.brightwave.example, or call extension 100.
- IT will verify your identity with your employee ID and your manager's name before resetting.
- A temporary password must be changed at the next login.

## 4. Laptops and Devices
- Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT and to security@brightwave.example within 1 hour.
- Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.
- Personal USB drives and external storage devices are not allowed on company laptops.
- Only IT-approved software may be installed. Request new software through a TechAssist ticket. Approval takes up to 3 business days.

## 5. Network and VPN
- Employees use the BW-Staff Wi-Fi network. Visitors use BW-Guest.
- Connect through the BrightLink VPN when working outside the office.

## 6. Phishing and Data Classification
- Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button in your email program. Do not click links or open attachments in them.
- Company data is classified as Public, Internal or Confidential. Confidential data must not be sent to personal email accounts or uploaded to unapproved cloud services.
