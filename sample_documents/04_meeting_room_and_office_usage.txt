# Office Rules, Meeting Room and Usage Guidelines
Brightwave Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version 1.5 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: This is a FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created only for testing a software project. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. Meeting Rooms
The head office has five bookable meeting rooms:
- Aurora: seats 4 people (2nd floor)
- Borealis: seats 6 people (2nd floor)
- Cirrus: seats 10 people (3rd floor)
- Delta: seats 16 people (3rd floor)
- Everest (boardroom): seats 24 people (4th floor)

## 2. Booking Rules
- Book rooms in the RoomBook calendar.
- The maximum booking is 2 hours at a time.
- A booking is released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes of the start time.
- Cancel at least 15 minutes before the start time if you no longer need the room.
- The Everest boardroom needs approval from the Office Administration team at least 2 working days in advance.
- No food is allowed in meeting rooms. Water and covered drinks are fine.

## 3. Meeting Etiquette
- Share the agenda at least 24 hours before the meeting.
- Start and finish on time. The default meeting length is 30 minutes.
- Send minutes and action items within 24 hours after the meeting.
- Take phone calls in the phone booths, not in open work areas.

## 4. Visitors
- All visitors must register at reception and show a photo ID.
- Visitors wear a visitor badge and must be escorted by an employee at all times.

## 5. Building Access and Parking
- The building is open from 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM. Working after 9:00 PM needs approval from the reporting manager and Office Administration.
- Two-wheeler parking is free. Car parking needs a permit from Office Administration.

## 6. Desk and Printing Rules
- Follow the clean desk rule: clear your desk and lock confidential papers at the end of the day.
- Printing is double-sided by default. Print only when necessary.
- Smoking is allowed only in the designated area outside the building.

## 7. Fire Safety
- The fire assembly point is Car Park A, in front of the main entrance.
- Evacuation drills are held every quarter. Do not use lifts during a fire alarm.
