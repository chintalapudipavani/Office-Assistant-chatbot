# New Employee Onboarding Guide
Brightwave Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version 1.3 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: This is a FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created only for testing a software project. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. Before Your First Day
- Return your signed offer letter to HR.
- Prepare your original photo ID proof, 2 passport-size photographs, and your bank account details for salary.

## 2. Day 1 Schedule
- Report to reception at 9:30 AM on your joining date.
- 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM: HR orientation and document verification.
- 1:00 PM: welcome lunch with your team.
- 2:00 PM: IT setup. You receive your laptop, headset and access card, and set up multi-factor authentication.

## 3. First Week Checklist
- Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
- Complete the security awareness training in the LearnHub portal within 5 days.
- Submit provident fund nomination forms to HR.
- Meet your onboarding buddy and your manager for a goal-setting talk.

## 4. Mandatory Training
- Code of Conduct training within 7 days of joining.
- Data Privacy training within 14 days of joining.
- Prevention of Sexual Harassment awareness training within 30 days of joining.

## 5. Buddy Program
- Every new employee is paired with a buddy for the first 90 days.
- The buddy helps with tools, team introductions and everyday questions.

## 6. Check-ins and Probation
- Check-ins with your manager take place at 30 days and 60 days.
- The probation review takes place at 90 days.

## 7. Salary and Benefits
- Salary is paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the PeopleDesk portal.
- Enrol yourself and your dependents in the group health insurance within 30 days of joining.

## 8. Who to Contact
- HR onboarding coordinator: Meera Nair (fictional), extension 215.
- IT help desk: extension 100.
- Office Administration: for access cards and parking permits.
