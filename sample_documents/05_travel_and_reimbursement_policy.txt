# Travel and Reimbursement Policy
Brightwave Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version 2.0 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: This is a FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created only for testing a software project. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. Approval Before Travel
- Get your manager's approval in the ExpenseHub portal before booking any business travel.
- International travel also needs approval from the department head and the Finance team, at least 14 days before departure.

## 2. Flights
- Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.
- Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and needs Vice President approval.
- Book domestic flights at least 7 days in advance.

## 3. Hotels
- The maximum hotel rate is INR 7,000 per night in Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru.
- The maximum hotel rate in all other cities is INR 4,500 per night.
- Minibar charges, laundry and in-room entertainment are not reimbursed.

## 4. Meals and Local Travel
- The daily meal allowance is INR 1,200 for domestic travel and USD 60 for international travel.
- Local taxi or cab rides are reimbursed with receipts.
- Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at INR 9 per km for cars and INR 4 per km for two-wheelers.
- Client entertainment is reimbursed up to INR 2,500 per person, with prior approval.

## 5. Travel Advance
- Employees may request a travel advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated trip cost.
- The advance must be settled within 7 days of returning from the trip.

## 6. Submitting Expense Claims
- Submit claims in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the expense or of returning from the trip.
- A receipt is required for every expense above INR 500.
- Claims submitted late need Vice President approval.
- Managers approve claims up to INR 10,000. Claims above INR 10,000 also need department head approval.
- Approved claims are paid by bank transfer within 10 working days.

## 7. Items That Are Not Reimbursed
- Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment and personal shopping.
- Upgrades that were not approved in advance.

## 8. Home Internet Allowance
- Employees eligible to work from home receive an internet allowance of INR 800 per month, paid with salary.
